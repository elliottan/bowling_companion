Headpin

A free bowling score keeper that works with no signal, needs no account, and keeps every game on your own phone.

Open Headpin

No sign up. Opens straight into a scorecard.

What it does

  • Keeps score. Tenpin, frame by frame, strikes and spares counted for you.
  • Works offline. Bowling alleys eat phone signal, so it never asks for any.
  • Installs to your home screen. Straight from the browser, no app store.
  • Tracks your arsenal. Which ball you threw, and which pins were left standing.
  • Remembers every night. Game history and averages over time.
  • Keeps it yours. No account, no ads, no tracking. Scores never leave the phone.
  • Backs up to a file. One you hold onto and can restore from anywhere.

How to install it

Open Headpin on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu and then Install app. It then opens like any other app, with or without signal.
