Install

Put it on your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open headpin.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with the arrow.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open headpin.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it asks, or the menu (⋮).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen.
