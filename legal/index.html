Privacy and terms

Last updated September 3, 2026

Headpin keeps your bowling scores on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no server holding your games.

The short version

Your scores, balls, lines and notes are saved in your browser's own storage on your device. Headpin has no backend, so they are never uploaded and nobody can read them.

The other side of that: nobody can recover them for you either. Export a backup from Settings, and keep it somewhere safe.

Privacy

What stays on your device

Everything you enter. Sessions, games, frames, shots, your arsenal, spare lines, oil patterns, lane notes and your settings are written to IndexedDB in your browser. They stay on that device until you delete them or the browser clears its storage.

Headpin does not ask for an account, an email address or a name. It uses no cookies and carries no advertising. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What does leave your device

Being honest about the exceptions matters more than the claim, so here they all are. Ball photos are not among them: all 446 of them are served from headpin.app itself, so looking at the catalog contacts no other site.

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, its servers record ordinary request logs, which include IP addresses. Once the app is installed it runs from your device's cache, so it usually only contacts the host to check for an update.
  • Feedback. "Send feedback" opens a draft email to hello@headpin.app in your own mail app. Headpin fills the body with your app version, install mode and how many rows are in each table, and puts the same text on your clipboard. It never includes your scores or the names of the places you bowl, it sends nothing by itself, and the draft sits in your mail app until you send it. A sent email carries your address, because that is how email works.
  • Page counts. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views, referrers and rough country-level location. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier in your browser, and never sees anything you enter in the app. It is there to answer one question: how many people opened Headpin. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Outbound links. Links to manufacturer pages and to "Buy me a coffee" take you to sites with their own policies.

Headpin is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your data and backups

Browser storage is not permanent. A browser can clear it when the device runs low on space, and it is erased if you clear site data, uninstall the app, or use private browsing. Headpin asks the browser to keep its storage persistent, but browsers are free to say no.

There is no copy of your scores anywhere else, and no way for anyone to restore them. Export a backup regularly from Settings, and keep the file somewhere you would keep a photo you care about.
